Navigating US Self-Employment Taxes in 2026

Operating as an independent contractor or 1099 freelancer in the United States requires a clear understanding of overhead burdens. Unlike W-2 employees where the employer pays half of Medicare and Social Security taxes, self-employed individuals are responsible for the entire 15.3% Self-Employment (SE) tax contribution.
